# Candidate Journey — Complete Guide

> End-to-end walkthrough of how a candidate experiences the GatiHire platform, from discovering a job to getting hired.

***

## 1. Discovering a Job

### How Candidates Find Jobs

Candidates find GatiHire jobs through multiple channels:

| Channel                | How It Works                                                              |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Job Boards**         | Jobs posted on Apna, Naukri, WorkIndia, and other boards link to GatiHire |
| **Direct Application** | Candidate visits the job's public page via a shared link                  |
| **Invite Link**        | Recruiter sends a personalized invite link via WhatsApp/email             |
| **GatiHire Portal**    | Candidate browses open positions on the GatiHire platform                 |

### The Public Job Page

When a candidate opens a job link, they see:

- Job title, company name, location
- Salary range, experience required
- Full job description
- Required skills
- **Apply Now** button

***

## 2. Applying for a Job

### One-Click Application

1. Candidate clicks **Apply Now** on the job page
2. If not logged in → redirected to sign up / log in
3. If logged in → application is submitted instantly
4. The candidate's profile is automatically linked to the job

### What Gets Submitted

- Candidate's full profile (name, skills, experience, education)
- Resume file (if uploaded during onboarding)
- Application timestamp and source

***

## 3. Resume Upload & Profile Creation

### First-Time Onboarding

When a candidate first signs up:

1. **Upload Resume** — drag & drop PDF/DOCX
2. **AI Parsing** — the system extracts:
   - Name, email, phone
   - Current role and company
   - Skills (technical and soft)
   - Work experience (companies, roles, durations)
   - Education (degree, university, year)
   - Key achievements and projects
3. **Profile Auto-Fill** — extracted data populates the candidate's profile
4. **Review & Edit** — candidate can review and adjust any field
5. **Save Profile** — profile is now complete and searchable

### Profile Components

| Section          | What's Stored                                                 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Basic Info**   | Name, email, phone, location, date of birth                   |
| **Professional** | Current role, desired role, total experience, current company |
| **Skills**       | Technical skills, soft skills, certifications, languages      |
| **Work History** | Previous companies, job titles, durations, achievements       |
| **Education**    | Degree, university, specialization, year, percentage          |
| **Preferences**  | Preferred location, salary expectation, notice period         |
| **Resume**       | Uploaded file (PDF/DOCX) + parsed text                        |

## 5. AI Screening Call

### What the Call Sounds Like

A friendly AI agent named "Bipul" calls the candidate:

**Inbound intro:**

> "Namaste \[Name], this is Bipul from Gati Hire. Thank you for applying for the \[Job Title] position. I'm calling to do a quick screening — it'll take about 5 minutes."

**Outbound intro:**

> "Namaste \[Name], this is Bipul from Gati Hire. We came across your profile and think you could be a great fit for a \[Job Title] role at \[Company]. Do you have a few minutes for a quick screening?"

### What Gets Discussed (rachit task: what to discuus)

The AI asks JD-specific questions:

1. **Experience**: "Can you tell me about your experience in \[relevant area]?"
2. **Current role**: "What are you currently doing at \[company]?"
3. **Salary expectation**: "What are your salary expectations for this role?"
4. **Notice period**: "When can you join if selected?"
5. **Availability**: "Are you open to working in \[location]?"
6. **Motivation**: "What interests you about this opportunity?"
7. Could you walk me through your current responsibilities as Manager of Rail Operations at Joshi Konoike?&#x20;
8. 2\. You've worked extensively with automobile rakes — tell me about your experience with rake planning, wagon allocation and NMG/BCACBM rakes.&#x20;
9. 3\. How do you coordinate with Indian Railways, freight forwarders and ICD/CFS operators, and how do you manage vendor performance?&#x20;
10. 4\. Describe a time you used GPS tracking and telematics (e.g. fleetedge, I-alert) to improve visibility and cut operating costs.&#x20;
11. 5\. How do you approach yard management, dispatch planning and inventory control to minimize dwell times and maximize throughput?&#x20;
12. 6\. What is your current CTC, and what would your expected compensation be for this role?&#x20;

### After the Call

The AI generates a structured verdict:

- **Score** (0-10): Overall fit assessment
- **Recommendation**: Advance / Further Review / Not a Fit
- **Strengths**: What the candidate did well
- **Concerns**: Any red flags or gaps
- **Key Answers**: Salary, notice period, experience summary

Candidates can see their application status on the GatiHire portal:

| Status            | What It Means                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Applied**       | Application received, pending review       |
| **AI Screening**  | Call scheduled or in progress              |
| **Under Review**  | AI screening complete, recruiter reviewing |

### Notifications

- **WhatsApp**: Status updates via WhatsApp messages
- **Email**: Formal notifications for major milestones
- **Portal**: Real-time status on the dashboard

## 7. Client Review

### What Happens

After the recruiter shortlists you:

1. Your profile is shared with the client company
2. The client reviews your:
   - AI screening results (strengths, concerns, recommendation)
   - Resume and experience
   - Salary expectations and availability
3. The client decides: **Approve** or **Pass**

### If Approved

- You move to the **Interview** stage
- The recruiter contacts you to schedule interviews
- You receive details about the interview format, timing, and location

### If Passed

- The recruiter informs you that the client decided to move in a different direction
- For inbound candidates: a professional rejection email is sent
- For outbound candidates: status updated silently (no outreach)

***

## 8. Interview Process

### Interview Rounds

Depending on the company's process:

| Round       | Typical Format                         |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Round 1** | Phone/video screen with hiring manager |
| **Round 2** | Technical/skills assessment            |
| **Round 3** | Final interview with leadership        |
| **Round 4** | Culture fit / HR round (if applicable) |

### After Each Round

- Recruiter records the outcome (Passed/Failed)
- You receive feedback or next steps
- If passed all rounds → moves to Offer stage

***

## 9. Offer & Hiring

### Receiving an Offer

1. The client extends an offer through the platform
2. You receive:
   - Offer letter details (role, salary, start date, benefits)
   - Instructions for accepting or negotiating
3. You can accept, negotiate, or decline

### Accepting the Offer

1. Accept the offer on the platform
2. Your status changes to **Hired**
3. You receive onboarding instructions from the company
4. The recruiter and client are notified

***

## 10. Candidate Rights & Data

### What We Store

- Resume and parsed profile data
- Application history
- AI screening transcripts and verdicts
- Communication history (WhatsApp, calls)

### What We Don't Store

- Payment information
- Social security / Aadhaar numbers
- Medical information

### Data Control

- Candidates can request profile updates at any time
- Candidates can request data deletion
- Resume files are stored securely in encrypted storage
- AI screening transcripts are retained for quality purposes
